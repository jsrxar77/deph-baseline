Six Minutes Of Cosmology
How long has it
been since your idea
of the order of
things had to tune
itself against something
larger than you?
This piece doesn't
illustrate an idea.
It builds one:
the cosmos as a
single composition,
tuned in fourths and
fifths, at 432 Hz,
the frequency it chose
to listen at.
The lights you're
watching turn on orbits
that turn.
Nothing returns to
exactly the same place.
Neither do you, while
you listen to this.
If something shifted,
stay for the full
piece. Subscribe for what
comes next.
