same template as the other three pieces — brand chip `deph · 04`, **GNOSIS** in Avenir Next Bold 200 px, a

resolution (3840x2160, lossless PNG intermediate) and downscaled with lanczos + a light sharpen, not
rendered directly at 1280x720 — Gnosis's 1-2 px lines turned to a soft blur at native 720p; supersampling
fixed it (found 2026-10-26).

**`gnosis-thumbnail.jpg` (chosen by the user) — 5:00, the octahedron.** Violet lace over the blue web,
with gold accents, moderate density. Several other candidates were built and rejected along the way: full
integration gold (9:20) read as one bright overexposed blob at a single frozen frame — great in motion,
not as a still; some early-Demiurgo moments showed a flat grey artifact from the form's wall mesh at low
presence (a separate, unresolved rendering bug, not pursued further since this candidate didn't need it).
Every discarded candidate file was deleted; only the chosen one is kept in the repo.
