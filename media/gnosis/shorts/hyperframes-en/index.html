Two Faces of the Same Energy

and being trapped
were just two faces
of the same energy?

untamed,
with no edges
to hold it back.
The other is
contained form,
rebuilding itself
again and again,
trying to
integrate.
Sound guides
the shape.
Every pulse at 432 Hz
transforms this image
in real
time.
And, for one instant,
the conflict disappears.
Does everything converge
into a single light?
